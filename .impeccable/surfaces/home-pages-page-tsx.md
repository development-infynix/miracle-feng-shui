# Homepage

Mode: Persuade. Storefront home for Miracle feng shui (feng shui decor, jewelry, candles, crystals and books; INR; the client previously sold on Etsy). Visitors: shoppers, many on phones, plus returning signed-in customers.

Requirements (user): New arrivals, 8 products (4 on phones) with View all; the 4 latest categories; a bento of Profile, Cart, Best sellers, Favorites and Socials (Instagram + Facebook platform cards). Order: headline, arrivals, categories, bento. Guests see sign-in invitations in Profile (primary pill), Cart and Favorites (outline pills).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet stone page where full-colour product photography carries the colour, set off by black ink, black pills and a bold brand serif. Refuses the marketplace banner-plus-rows template and the red-and-gold festive feng shui default.

OWN-WORLD: Warm stone ground #EEEBE3, black ink #141210, muted ink #5E5A52, black full-pill primary and outline secondary, flat white tiles with 10px corners and hairline borders, hairline black rules above section headings. Display type is the brand wordmark serif (Charter / Cambria stack) in bold; Geist for everything else. Logo purple #431769 only for small marks (logo, avatar, count badges) and hover text. Gold #E7A93B only as a soft hover shadow under black pills and category tiles. Navbar is an open bar on the page that gains a stone backing on scroll, with a black Cart pill; footer is a stone glass sheet.

STORY: The visitor reads one line saying what the shop is for, sees what's new, picks a category, then finds their account, cart, favorites, the best sellers and the shop's socials in one bento close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width bold serif headline, "Bring wealth, calm and protection home.", capped at 96px, with no description or buttons (user decision); the New arrivals heading with View all and the first product row begin inside the first screen on desktop.

FORM: User-pinned direction "Stone & Gold" from the user's reference image, adjusted by the user: lime replaced by logo purple, intro copy and buttons removed, pointer glow removed, brand serif instead of Young Serif. No ambient motion; image hover zooms and the gold hover shadow are the only motion. seed: d8982837d40fca4d

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
