## Platform

web

## Users

Miracle Feng Shui is an online store selling feng shui decor, jewelry, candles, crystals and books, with prices in Indian rupees.

**Primary admin user:** the owner, running the shop solo. The owner handles orders, packing, stock and the catalog, and opens the admin first each day.

**Customers:** shoppers browsing the storefront. They can pay online through Razorpay or by cash on delivery.

## Admin Dashboard Jobs

When the owner opens the dashboard, it answers these questions:

1. What needs action now?
2. How are sales going?
3. What is selling?
4. Is the catalog ready?

## Operating Context

- The store is live, and real orders exist in a database other than the local development database.
- The local development database can have no orders, so every admin view must stay truthful and useful when empty.

## Terminology

Use the terms already in the product: Products Catalog, Customer Orders, Categories and Customer Users.
