# Illustration credits

- `peep-*.svg`: figures from **Open Peeps** by Pablo Stanley (CC0, https://www.openpeeps.com), rendered to static SVG with the `react-peeps` package (MIT) and recoloured to the Stone & Gold palette (stone lines on logo purple or charcoal).
- `cart-bag.svg`: hand-drawn for this site; a shopping bag with a candle, a crystal and a money tree sprig, and a gold lucky coin tag, stone line on charcoal.
- `heart-brush.svg`: drawn for this site; one dark ink brush-stroke heart (generated as a variable-width stroke with a dry-brush edge filter), after the owner's sketch.
- `explore-bestsellers.svg`: drawn for this site for the guest "Explore the shop" tile: a prize rosette, stone line on charcoal with gold accents. The tile's other two cards use the shop's logo (`/images/logofull.svg`) and the owner-supplied `/images/all-categories-card.svg`.
